# MediGuard 🏥

**MediGuard** is a smart, AI-powered healthcare management web application designed to provide users with quick and accessible health assistance. The platform helps users analyze symptoms, assess potential health risks, understand possible causes, and access useful healthcare information through an easy-to-use interface.

### ✨ Key Features

* 🩺 **AI-Based Health Risk Assessment** – Analyze user-provided symptoms and identify potential health risks.
* 🔍 **Symptom Analysis** – Provides health insights based on the symptoms entered by the user.
* 📊 **Health Risk Prediction** – Categorizes potential health risks and provides understandable results.
* 🚨 **Emergency Assistance** – Helps users identify potentially urgent situations and provides quick guidance.
* 📄 **Health Report Generation** – Generate and access health-related reports based on the assessment.
* 🏥 **Nearby Healthcare Facilities** – Helps users find nearby hospitals and healthcare facilities.
* 🌐 **User-Friendly Interface** – Designed with a simple and accessible interface for better user experience.

### 🎯 Objective

The main objective of MediGuard is to make preliminary healthcare assistance more accessible by combining **AI-based analysis, health information, and digital healthcare utilities** into a single platform.

> **Note:** MediGuard is intended for preliminary health assistance and awareness. It does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
